Warm-spiced chicken and chickpeas with apricots and olives. Sweet-savory, and the sauce is thin enough to soak straight into quinoa.

Notes:
- The apricots are doing structural work here — they melt down and sweeten the sauce, so don't skip them. Prunes or dates work as substitutes.
- If you want it richer, stir 2 tablespoons of tomato paste in with the spices.
- Storage: 4 days in the fridge, 3 months frozen; keep the quinoa in separate containers.
- Olives can get salty over time in the fridge, so hold back on salt at the end if you're batching for the week.
- A spoonful of plain yogurt on top when reheating is very good.
